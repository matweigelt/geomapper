Choosing a projection

The model is a SPHERE, not an ellipsoid. Every projection here uses the authalic radius 6371.0072 km. The resulting geometric error is at most about 0.3%, which is invisible in a figure and unacceptable in a survey. This is a visualisation tool, not a survey tool. If you need coordinates to hold on the ground, use a projection library with an ellipsoid.

The rule behind the table

Ask what the reader will MEASURE off the figure. If they will compare areas - a mass anomaly, a basin total, an ice loss - the projection must be equal-area or the comparison is wrong before it starts. If they will read angles or shapes, it must be conformal. No projection is both, and a compromise projection is neither.

Every projection's exact domain, singularities and clip limits are queryable: geo.crs(name).Domain.
